Understanding the Challenge

Why Antenatal Depression
Screening Matters

Antenatal depression affects 10–20% of pregnant women worldwide, yet systematic screening remains inconsistent, especially in resource-constrained settings.

10–25% Prevalence

Up to 25% of pregnant women in LMICs experience clinically significant depressive symptoms during pregnancy.

3–5× Postnatal Risk

Untreated antenatal depression increases the probability of developing postnatal depression by 3 to 5 fold.

Adverse Outcomes

Preterm birth (OR 1.37), low birth weight (OR 1.28), and compromised maternal-infant bonding are linked to untreated AD.

Screening Gap

Routine screening is frequently omitted in resource-constrained settings due to workforce limitations and lack of infrastructure.

Research Challenges Addressed

C1

Domain Structure Ignorance

Existing ML models treat clinical features as flat, exchangeable input vectors, discarding intra-domain correlations.

C2

Temporal Insensitivity

No widely adopted model explicitly captures trimester-specific depression risk evolution during pregnancy.

C3

Class Imbalance

Models with standard cross-entropy loss systematically miss depressed patients — the ones needing clinical attention.

C4

Interpretability Deficit

Clinical adoption requires transparent, actionable predictions — not opaque probability scores.

C5

Cross-Cohort Robustness Gap

Models developed on single datasets may not transfer reliably across diverse cultural and healthcare contexts.

Model Architecture

TH-DAT
Architecture Pipeline

Five hierarchical, progressively abstracting components with ~1.69M trainable parameters.

C1

Domain-Grouped Feature Tokenizer

Each scalar feature is projected into 128-dim space via feature-specific linear transformations, augmented with positional + domain embeddings.

d=128 N=19 features 3 domains
C2

Global Transformer Encoder

6-layer, 8-head transformer with trimester conditioning (α=0.1) enabling intra- and cross-domain feature interactions.

L=6 layers H=8 heads d_ff=512
C3

Domain-Aware Attention Pooling

3 learnable query vectors attend over domain-specific tokens to produce domain summary matrix S ∈ ℝ3×d.

Demo Obst Psych
C4

Trimester Cross-Attention

Trimester embedding queries domain summaries — learning trimester-specific attention patterns over clinical domains.

T1: ≤13w T2: 14–26w T3: ≥27w
C5

Gated Domain Fusion

Per-patient softmax gate weights with entropy regularization — interpretable domain contributions summing to 1.

g = softmax(MLP) Σgₖ = 1

Two-Phase Training Strategy

Phase 1

Self-Supervised Masked Pretraining

  • 80 epochs of masked feature reconstruction
  • Random 20% feature masking → MSE reconstruction
  • Learns inter-feature statistical dependencies
  • No depression labels used in this phase
Phase 2

Supervised Fine-tuning

  • 300 epochs with focal + contrastive + entropy loss
  • AdamW optimizer (lr=5e-4)
  • MixUp augmentation (α=0.2, 50% prob)
  • SWA after epoch 200, gradient clipping at 1.0

Composite Loss Function

Ltotal = Lfocal + 0.03 · Lcontrastive + 0.01 · Lentropy
Focal Loss γ=2.0, focuses on hard boundary cases
Contrastive Loss τ=0.1, pulls same-class representations together
Entropy Reg. Prevents domain mode collapse
